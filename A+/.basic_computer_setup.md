## 1. How a Computer Works: Data Flow

Every task on a computer follows a continuous teamwork loop between hardware components

`Input` === *(Instruction)* ===> ` Processing & Storage ` === *(Result)* ===> `Output`

--- 

## 2. Essential Hardware Components

* **CPU (Central Processing Unit) + Cooler:** The primary processor that executes program instructions and math operations, paired with a heatsink/fan or liquid cooler to prevent thermal throttling.
* **Motherboard:** The central printed circuit board (PCB) housing the CPU socket, chipset, RAM slots, and power delivery circuits that allow all parts to communicate.
* **RAM (Random Access Memory):** High-speed, volatile memory that temporarily holds active data for quick CPU access (clears when powered off).
* **GPU (Graphics Processing Unit):** Dedicated hardware for parallel visual processing, 3D rendering, and video acceleration (can be integrated inside the CPU or installed as a standalone card).
* **Storage Drives (NVMe SSD / SATA SSD / HDD):** Non-volatile storage devices that keep data permanently. SSDs use fast flash memory; HDDs use spinning magnetic platters.
* **PSU (Power Supply Unit):** Converts wall AC electricity into regulated low-voltage DC power for internal components.
* **Computer Case (Chassis):** The structural enclosure that mounts components, protects against dust, and directs cooling airflow.

### Recommended Workspace & Tools
* **Magnetic Phillips Screwdriver:** Prevents dropping small screws onto sensitive circuitry.
* **Parts Tray / Small Bowl:** Keeps motherboard, case, and drive screws separated and organized.
* **Clean, Non-Conductive Flat Surface:** A wooden or plastic desk (avoid working on carpet to prevent static electricity/ESD damage, and avoid bare metal surfaces).

## 3. Step-by-Step PC Assembly & Setup

### Phase 1: PC Assembly (Outside the Case)
1. **Prepare the Motherboard:** Place the motherboard on top of its cardboard box to protect the solder points underneath.
2. **Install the CPU:** Lift the socket retention lever, align the triangle indicator on the CPU corner with the socket mark, gently seat the chip without forcing it, and lock the lever.
3. **Install Storage:** Slide the M.2 SSD into its motherboard slot at a 30-degree angle and secure it with its retaining screw (do this before installing large air coolers).
4. **Mount the CPU Cooler:** Ensure thermal paste is applied, align the cooler mounting brackets over the CPU, tighten screws in a cross (`X`) pattern for even pressure, and plug the fan cable into the `CPU_FAN` header.
5. **Install RAM:** Open the DIMM slot latches (typically slots 2 and 4 for dual-channel performance), align the notch on the RAM stick, and press firmly until the latches click into place.

### Phase 2: Chassis Installation & Wiring
6. **Prepare the Case:** Remove both side panels, lay the case flat, and install the **PSU** into its bottom bay with the fan facing the intake vent.
7. **Mount the Motherboard:** Align the motherboard's rear I/O ports with the back of the case, seat it onto the brass standoffs, and secure it with screws (snug, not over-tightened).
8. **Connect Power Cables:** Plug in the 24-pin ATX motherboard power cable, the 8-pin CPU power cable (top-left of the board), and SATA cables if using 2.5" SSDs or 3.5" HDDs.
9. **Connect Front Panel Headers:** Attach the case's power switch, reset switch, front USB, and audio cables to the bottom pins of the motherboard using the motherboard manual's pinout diagram.
10. **Install Dedicated GPU (If Applicable):** Remove the corresponding rear PCIe slot covers, insert the GPU firmly into the top `PCIe x16` slot until the latch clicks, screw it to the chassis, and attach its PCIe power cables from the PSU.
11. **Cable Management & Closure:** Route excess cables behind the motherboard tray so airflow remains unobstructed, then reattach the side panels.

### Phase 3: First Boot & OS Installation
12. **Connect Peripherals:** Connect the monitor cable directly to the **GPU ports** (not the motherboard ports, if a GPU is installed), then plug in the keyboard and mouse.
13. **Power On & Enter BIOS:** Flip the PSU switch on, press the case power button, and tap `Delete` or `F2` during startup to open the **BIOS/UEFI**. Verify that the CPU temperature is stable and all RAM and storage drives are detected.
14. **Install the Operating System:** Insert a bootable USB drive containing your OS installer (e.g., Windows or Linux), save and exit the BIOS and folllow the installation prompts.

## 4. System Configurations by Workload

| Use Case | Primary Demands | Hardware Priorities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **School / Office** | Document editing, web browsing, research, video conferencing. | **Mid-range CPU (with Integrated Graphics), 16GB RAM, 512GB NVMe SSD.** No dedicated GPU is required, keeping cost and power usage low. |
| **Gaming** | Real-time 3D rendering, high frame rates, physics simulation. | **High-tier Dedicated GPU, fast single-core CPU, 16GB–32GB RAM, 1TB+ NVMe SSD.** Requires strong airflow and a reliable, higher-wattage PSU. |
| **Video Editing** | 4K/8K timeline scrubbing, color grading, encoding/exporting. | **High-core-count CPU, 32GB–64GB+ RAM, capable Dedicated GPU (for hardware acceleration), and dual fast SSDs** (one for OS/apps, one for raw media scratch files). |
